Lesson 7 · 17 min

Ethics, risk and written authorization

A client request can take down their entire site: a network of sites, a thousand articles, forced indexing. You need to refuse by name, explain the risk and offer the safe path. And before touching anything, get in writing what you are allowed to do. An SEO diagnosis reads the site the way a search engine does: it is not a pentest.

02 · The tactics and what to offer instead

Tap each step.

1. Scaled content

Five variations per keyword, a thousand articles, agents publishing 24 hours a day: this is what Google calls scaled content abuse.

"Humanizing" the text to pass an AI detector doesn't fix it: it aims at the wrong metric (Google evaluates usefulness) and it is paraphrasing at scale with intent to deceive.

At INEMA283 entries of 170 to 240 words, two of them with 47% of their vocabulary in common, published in a single commit. Of the 6 in the sample, 5 were never crawled.

2. Linked networks

A network of your own sites with cross-links and "20 backlinks per post" is a link scheme. One manual action can take down all the domains at once.

Instead: contextual links, 1 or 2 per page, and real mentions (a partner, an event, an open project).

3. Shortcuts

A subreddit named after the keyword, or mass posts on LinkedIn to rank, is site reputation abuse. The Google Indexing API is only for job postings (JobPosting) and live streams (BroadcastEvent); a paid indexer is a typical spam tool.

Instead: sitemap, Search Console, IndexNow and internal links.

4. Not a pentest

The X-ray reads public pages the way a search engine does: GET, a pause between pages, no passwords, forms or vulnerability scans.

If the client wants security, that is another service, with another authorization and another professional. Say so in the proposal.

03 · Written authorization template

A template to adapt. It is not legal advice: for a large contract, run it by a lawyer.

SEO service authorization
AUTHORIZATION — [client] authorizes [you/company] — [date]

1. SCOPE: SEO and AI visibility diagnosis and monitoring
   of the sites: [domain 1], [domain 2]. Nothing outside this list.
2. WHAT IS READ: public pages (the way a search engine reads them, with a pause between pages).
   NOT A PENTEST: no password testing, forms, intrusion or search for security flaws.
3. ACCESS (minimum):
   - Search Console and Bing Webmaster: read-only user, in the name of [you].
   - DNS/domain: only to verify ownership, done by [client] or in their presence.
   - No passwords by message. Credentials stay out of repositories and reports.
4. WHAT CAN BE CHANGED: [e.g.: titles, sitemap, robots.txt, approved new pages].
   Every publication goes through approval by [name].
5. PERSONAL DATA: reports use aggregated data from the dashboards; no data from
   visitors or end customers is collected or exposed (LGPD).
6. WILL NOT BE DONE: network of sites, buying links, paid indexer, Indexing API outside
   permitted use, scaled content without review.
7. VALIDITY: until [date]. It can be revoked at any time in writing;
   upon revocation, access is removed within [N] days.

Signatures: [client] · [you]
Table: request → I decline → I offer
REQUEST                         | WHY I DECLINE                        | WHAT I OFFER
1,000 AI articles               | scaled content (spam policy)         | a few pages with original data
5 sites linking to each other   | link scheme; manual action           | contextual links and real mentions
"forcing" indexing              | Indexing API only JobPosting/Broadcast | sitemap, Search Console, IndexNow
text that passes a detector     | aims at the wrong metric             | human review and original data
"example" testimonials          | fabricated trust signal              | only real numbers with a source

04 · Practice: the paperwork before the work

The two documents that go with every proposal.

E1 · Completed authorization

30 min

Fill in the authorization template for the client from lesson 4 (or for your own site, as client zero).

Done when: all 7 items are filled in, the domains are listed one by one, the access is read-only, and the phrase "not a pentest" is in item 2.

E2 · Request, refusal, offer

20 min

Build your own table with 5 requests you have heard or expect to hear, the reason for declining, and what you offer instead.

Done when: each row names the tactic (scaled content, link scheme, site reputation abuse, forced indexing or fabricated signal) and has an alternative you can actually deliver.
