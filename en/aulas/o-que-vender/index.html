Lesson 1 · 15 min

What to sell: a measured non-branded result, not position 1

The client comes asking for first place on Google, 500 articles, or to "show up in ChatGPT." Nobody controls that, and whoever promises it loses the client in month 6. What you can sell is what you control and can prove: diagnosis, plan, measured follow-up, and training.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Why building got easy and trust didn't
  2. 2The promises you don't make, and what to offer instead
  3. 3The four deliverables you can sell and prove
  4. 4Why the number that proves the work is the non-branded one

Goal: separate what the client asks for from what you can deliver and measure, and write your offer of diagnosis, plan, follow-up, and training.

02 · Why sell this way

Tap each step.

1. Building got easy

The client can already generate a site, a page, and text with AI. Selling only the build turns into a price fight.

What they can't do alone is know whether it works, what is wrong, and what to do first.

2. Trust is the product

The company buys someone who diagnoses, reviews, tracks, and trains, and who says clearly what they know and what they don't know how to do.

In SEO this becomes: diagnosis, plan, measured follow-up, and training.

3. The total is misleading

In the 28-day baseline for inema.club, 639 of the 647 clicks from the top 25 searches were branded. Non-branded searches added up to 8 clicks.

If you show the client only the total, you're showing people who already knew them.

At INEMAIn four weeks, inema.club's non-branded impressions went from 8 to 157. Non-branded clicks stayed at 0. That's the kind of honest number you report.

4. What not to do

A network of cross-linked sites, a paid indexer, Google Indexing API for ordinary articles, one page per keyword variation.

A manual action takes down all of the client's domains at once. Lesson 7 covers each one in detail.

03 · Templates for your offer

Copy it and swap what's in [brackets] for your own.

Positioning in 2 lines
I help [type of business] get found by people who don't know the brand yet, on Google, on Bing and in AI answers.
I do the diagnosis, the plan, measured monthly follow-up and team training. I don't promise rankings, I don't sell article packages and I don't guarantee AI citations.
Swapping a promise for a deliverable
CLIENT REQUEST                 -> WHAT I OFFER
"first place on Google"        -> diagnosis + plan + non-branded number measured every week
"500 articles with AI"         -> a few pages with original data, at a pace the site can handle
"show up on ChatGPT"           -> a fixed question set, run by hand every month, with a spreadsheet
"AI score" from a tool         -> official dashboards: Search Console and Bing Webmaster
"llms.txt fixes it"            -> optional, cheap item in the foundation, not the main deliverable

04 · Practice: your offer on paper

Two short files you'll reuse throughout the course.

E1 · Positioning in 2 lines

20 min

Write your positioning from the template: who it's for, what you deliver and what you don't do.

Done when: the 2 lines don't name any tool, they name the four deliverables (or the ones you do), and they say at least one thing you don't promise.

E2 · Five promises swapped

25 min

List 5 requests you've heard (or imagine hearing) from a client and, next to each one, write the measurable deliverable you offer instead.

Done when: each of the 5 lines has a request and a deliverable with a number you can check (non-branded, indexed, questions where you appear).
