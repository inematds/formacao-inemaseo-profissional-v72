Lesson 8 · 20 min

Prospecting and combined offer: from teaching to ongoing support

Selling website building has become a price fight. What sells well is a ladder: teach, diagnose, support, and review, all the way to the monthly report. Prospecting changes too: you show up with a public finding about the person's site, not a package. And your own site, measured honestly, is the best proof.

02 · Combined offer and prospecting

Tap each step.

1. Combined offer

One diagnostic covers it all: what search engines see, the question set run on the AIs, and readiness for agents. A new site, when you need one, comes in as part of the plan, not as the product.

WebMCP is preparation: a W3C community draft (document.modelContext), with no stable browser. It is not a ranking factor.

2. Finding prospects

Well-reviewed businesses whose site has a problem you can see from the outside: a page with no text in the HTML, mixed canonicals, search bots blocked.

Write down the finding and the URL. It's the reason for reaching out, and it's already a sample of the diagnostic.

Prospector Agent ideaINEMA's open pipeline runs from discovered → qualified → … → gate → sent, with opt-out, a suppression list and one contact per lead. Use its discipline; swap the site redesign for the diagnostic.

3. First contact

One message: the finding, why it matters, and the offer to show the diagnostic. No promise of rankings, with an option to stop receiving messages.

One contact per prospect. If there's no reply, don't push with an automatic sequence.

4. Your site is the proof

Apply the method to your own site and publish the before and after, including what didn't work.

INEMA tells its own zero: 0 of 52 AI measurements in July, non-branded impressions from 8 to 157, non-branded clicks still at 0 on the club. That convinces more than a testimonial.
